About Reveuse Treats

A cosy home bakery creating dreamy, customised treats on pre-order.

Reveuse Treats is a home-based bakery where every order is crafted fresh only for you. There is no display counter, no bulk production – just thoughtful, small-batch bakes made with care.

From pastel cakes and bento boxes to cupcakes with tiny messages, each treat is customised to match your theme, mood and occasion. You share your idea, and we bring it to life in frosting.

As a pre-order only setup, we work with limited slots so we can focus fully on quality, detailing and clean flavours in every box that leaves the kitchen.
